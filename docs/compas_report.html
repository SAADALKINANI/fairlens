What the criteria mean

  • Demographic / statistical parity (pprev): Each group is flagged at a similar rate.
  • Proportional parity (ppr): Each group's share of all flags is similar.
  • False positive rate parity (fpr): Innocent / negative people are wrongly flagged at similar rates.
  • False negative rate parity (fnr): People who need help / are positive are missed at similar rates.
  • Predictive parity (precision): A flag means the same thing for every group.
  • False discovery rate parity (fdr): Flags are wrong at similar rates.
  • False omission rate parity (for): Non-flags are wrong at similar rates.

Generated by fairlens. Fairness criteria can conflict mathematically when base rates differ between groups; choose the ones that match the harm you care about.
